# Verification: club and collection

**How to run this file.**
- **Start state.** Start each section in a fresh browser profile on the production build (see [the protocol](README.md#how-to-run-a-pass)), with Arena settings at their defaults: **Reduced motion** and **Lower graphics quality** unticked, and the scoring policy at win 3, draw 1, loss 0, **Entries / User** 4, **Counted entries enabled** ticked.
- **Fresh save.** A fresh save already holds the two basketball counted contests: Doug beat Dan 2–0, and Sam and Riley drew 1–1. So Standings start at Doug 3, Riley 1, Sam 1, Dan 0.
- **Between sections.** Use **Reset demo…** and **Reset demo data** to return the Arena save to the fixtures. Reset does not remove installed characters or Play bindings, so for **Install character** items clear the site's IndexedDB `wybmh-character-library-v1` from developer tools, or use a new profile.
- **Device column:**
  - **mouse** and **keyboard** mean ordinary page use.
  - **two tabs** means two tabs of the same profile.
  - **storage** means developer tools, to read or change localStorage and IndexedDB.
- **Character packs.** Items needing one say "a valid pack": a `.arena-character.json` made by the workflow in `docs/ADDING-CHARACTERS.md`.

Not checkable by hand:

- **The warning's scope.** It mentions contests, awards and mappings but not the scoring policy or the waiting contest; whether it should is a product call ([What a reset replaces and keeps](../club/reset-demo.md#what-a-reset-replaces-and-keeps), [Open questions and verification](../club/reset-demo.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- **Waiting for another tab's locking write.** The reset queues behind a lock that lasts a fraction of a second; landing a reset inside it cannot be arranged on demand ([Committing](../club/reset-demo.md#committing)).
- **A write cut off between its steps** being recovered on the next load, because the revision rises rather than restarting at zero, needs a write interrupted at a chosen instant ([Cancel and interrupt](../club/reset-demo.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- **The format error's advice.** "This save has an unsupported format. Export it before resetting." needs a save with a valid checksum and an unsupported format, which cannot be written by hand ([Edge cases](../club/reset-demo.md#edge-cases)).
- **`configure_arena_event` behind the dialog** needs a browser that offers WebMCP, and belongs to the agent tools checklist ([Cancel and interrupt](../club/reset-demo.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- **Focus after the swap.** The document makes no claim about where focus goes when **Reset demo…** replaces the dialog's content, or after it closes ([Open questions and verification](../club/reset-demo.md#open-questions-and-verification)).

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether the dialog should start fresh rather than show the last review or error is a product call ([Open questions](../collection/install-character.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- How a card becomes a pack in Codex is outside the product description ([Summary](../collection/install-character.md#summary)).
- `configure_arena_event` switching views under the dialog, abandoning a check or letting a save finish, needs a browser that offers WebMCP; the cross-cutting agent-tool items use an emulation ([Cancel and interrupt](../collection/install-character.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- That the library write is all or nothing cannot be interrupted on demand: a reload during the brief save cannot be timed reliably ([Cancel and interrupt](../collection/install-character.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- Most rows of [What is checked](../collection/install-character.md#what-is-checked) (markers, sizes, transparency, the creation record, the motion profile, the artwork paths) need packs altered in ways that also break their checksums or artwork paths, which takes a pack-making tool rather than a text editor. INSTALL-18 to INSTALL-22 cover the ones a tester can produce by hand.
- That images are opened one at a time "to bound memory on phones" is not observable ([What is checked](../collection/install-character.md#what-is-checked)).
- Privacy-hardened browsers that disturb canvas pixels failing the transparency check are outside the default configuration ([Open questions](../collection/install-character.md#open-questions-and-verification)).

Not checkable by hand:

- There is no way to see or remove a single mapping; the text area always shows the example, and only **Reset demo** removes mappings. Whether the page should offer either is a product call ([Open questions](../collection/asset-mapping.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- A mapping's motion profile can change cornhole scores in later contests, including counted entries. Whether a presentation mapping should be able to do that is a product call, and a Watch outcome cannot be predicted to show it ([What a mapping changes](../collection/asset-mapping.md#what-a-mapping-changes); [Open questions](../collection/asset-mapping.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- That later recordings take each throw's release point and each attempt's timing from the mapping cannot be told apart by eye from an unmapped contest ([What a mapping changes](../collection/asset-mapping.md#what-a-mapping-changes)).
- Play taking the mapping at **Start** shows nothing visible for Dan and Doug, whose Play figures are built in ([What a mapping changes](../collection/asset-mapping.md#what-a-mapping-changes)).
- The cross-tab lock that serializes mapping writes cannot be exercised on demand: two writes would have to land at the same moment ([Committing](../collection/asset-mapping.md#committing)).
- `configure_arena_event` switching to Watch while the dialog is open needs a browser that offers WebMCP; the cross-cutting agent-tool items use an emulation ([Cancel and interrupt](../collection/asset-mapping.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
