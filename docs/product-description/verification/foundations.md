# Verification: foundations

How to run this file: serve the production build of commit `364e3c1` (see [How to run a pass](README.md#how-to-run-a-pass)) and open `http://127.0.0.1:3020/` in a fresh browser profile on a machine with a real GPU; software WebGL runs game time and playback time several times slower than wall time, so every timing item is meaningless there. Every item starts from a fresh save in a fresh profile unless its Setup says otherwise: no `wybmh-paper-arena-v2`, `wybmh-input-bindings-v2` or `wybmh-input-bindings-v1` in localStorage and no `wybmh-character-library-v1` in IndexedDB. Keep these preferences at their defaults: the operating system's "reduce motion" setting off (the page's **Reduced motion** starts from it), **Lower graphics quality** off, Watch and Play sound off, the clean spectator view off, browser zoom 100%, the window about 1440×1000 unless the item says otherwise, Num Lock on, and no controller plugged in unless the Device column says `controller`. Between sections, and after any item that writes the save, bindings or library, close the profile or clear those stores in developer tools and reload. Shorthand used below: **Play defaults** is the Play tab just opened (Cornhole, **PLAYER 1** Doug on **Keyboard · WASD**, **PLAYER 2** Dan on **AI player**); **stage focused** means the match has finished loading and nothing outside the stage has been clicked since, or the tester has clicked the stage's drawing or pressed Tab from **Back to setup** to reach the stage; **cornhole aiming** means the 1.45 s entrances are over and player 1, who throws first by slot order, is aiming. Device values: `mouse` is pointer clicks; `keyboard` is a physical keyboard, with a numeric keypad for keyboard 2 items, pressed while the stage is focused unless the steps say otherwise; `controller` is a real Xbox, PlayStation or generic pad with a button pressed on the page before **Start**, except that items needing an exact analog value (0.1, 0.15, 0.18, 0.2, 0.55) use the emulated controller of `tests/browser/input.spec.ts`, because nobody can hold a trigger at an exact fraction by hand, and are recorded as emulated; `touch` is a touch screen or the on-screen controls clicked with a mouse; `two tabs` is two tabs of the same profile (they share one save); `hidden tab` is switching to another browser tab, which is not the same as `blur`, clicking into another application window; `storage` is developer tools, to read or edit localStorage and IndexedDB, block storage, or run a console command (focusing developer tools blurs the page window, which pauses a Play match, so click back into the page before continuing); `small screen` is responsive mode at the width the item gives.

Not checkable by hand:

- The opponent always playing **Steady**: the page never shows the opponent's strategy ([Kinds of play](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#kinds-of-play)).
- Extra pairs and the unresolved draw: a tie after the regulation attempts cannot be produced on demand, and a tie after three extra pairs even less so. A tester can only lock exhibitions with **Up to 3 extra equal pairs** until one happens (see also B-31) ([Kinds of play](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#kinds-of-play)).
- How traits, the card's shot tendencies, poses and personality, and an attached asset mapping shape outcomes. These are statistical, or need two recordings with the same seed and different cards; rarity giving no advantage is a design statement ([How a contest is decided](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#how-a-contest-is-decided)).
- A recording that fails its own integrity checks being refused: nothing on the page can produce one ([How a contest is decided](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#how-a-contest-is-decided)).
- The refusals "No counted entry remains for this event. Choose Exhibition.", "This ranked entry is unavailable." and "A competitor has used their ranked allowance. Exhibition is still available.": the dialog disables **Start showdown** first, so they need the save to change in another tab in the instant before the press ([Backing out at once](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#backing-out-at-once)).
- A reload during **Locking the contest…**: the lock takes well under a second, so the reload cannot be timed to land inside it ([Open questions and verification](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- Locking being one journal-protected write, so that a crash keeps the whole contest or none of it: a crash between the write's steps cannot be produced by hand; `tests/run-tests.mjs` covers the journal ([Interactions with other systems](../foundations/contests-and-recordings.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).

Not checkable by hand:

- The format check's reason in the recovery box, "This browser’s Arena save could not be read: This save has an unsupported format. Export it before resetting.", and the refusal of a save holding a duplicate award: both need a hand-made save whose checksum is correct, which cannot be computed by hand ([Edge cases](../foundations/saved-data.md#edge-cases)).
- A write interrupted between its steps leaving a journal that the next load recovers: the three steps run in one moment and cannot be interrupted on demand. SAVE-06 checks the recovery from a staged journal ([Cancel and interrupt](../foundations/saved-data.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- A write already under way finishing and not being cancellable: writes finish too quickly to act during one ([Cancel and interrupt](../foundations/saved-data.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- Whether a playback-position write, which takes no cross-tab lock, can overwrite a contest locked a moment earlier in another tab. The window is a few milliseconds and cannot be hit on demand ([Open questions and verification](../foundations/saved-data.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- Club points and standings being recomputed from the ledger with no stored total: a design statement; the save simply holds no total ([Interactions with other systems](../foundations/saved-data.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).

- Many tabs with stages open making a lost graphics context more likely: it depends on the GPU and the browser's limits, and cannot be produced reliably ([Interactions with other systems](../foundations/stage.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).
