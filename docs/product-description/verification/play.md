# Verification: Play

**How to run this file.**
- **Start state.** Start each section in a fresh browser profile on the production build (see [the protocol](README.md#how-to-run-a-pass)), with Arena settings at their defaults: **Reduced motion** and **Lower graphics quality** unticked. Open **Play**.
- **Setup.** Unless an item says otherwise, use the default setup: **Cornhole**, player 1 on **Keyboard · WASD** with the Doug card, player 2 an **AI player** with the Dan card.
- **Focus.** Before pressing game keys, make sure the stage has keyboard focus. It takes focus when the match opens. Otherwise, Tab to it, or use **Pause game** and **Resume game**; clicking the stage's drawing does not focus it.
- **Between sections.** Leave the match with **Back to setup**, or reload for a clean match. Clear `wybmh-input-bindings-v1` between remapping items.
- **Device column:**
  - **keyboard** means a physical keyboard. Keyboard 2 items need a numeric keypad.
  - **controller** means a real Xbox, PlayStation or generic pad. Press one of its buttons on the page before **Start**, because browsers reveal a pad only after that.
  - **touch** means a touch screen, or the on-screen controls clicked with a mouse where the item allows.
  - **blur** means switching to another application window.
  - **hidden tab** means switching to another browser tab.
- **Timing items.** These need a real machine at normal frame rate. Under software rendering, game time runs slow.

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether a quick tap should throw a dud rather than do nothing, unlike the on-screen controls. This is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/cornhole.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- Scatter size, ±11 × (1 − accuracy) px, the Play accuracies (Dan 0.76, Doug 0.68), and precision mode narrowing scatter to 40% only if it is still active when the bag leaves the hand. Scatter is random for each throw, so checking it needs many throws measured in stage pixels ([The throw](../play/cornhole.md#the-throw), [Modifiers](../play/cornhole.md#modifiers)).
- That each match draws a fresh random seed, so the same inputs do not repeat the same scatter. This cannot be seen from one match ([Interactions with other systems](../play/cornhole.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).
- A lost WebGL context during a match. Play has no handler, the outcome is unknown, and producing the loss needs a script against the canvas ([Cancel and interrupt](../play/cornhole.md#cancel-and-interrupt), [Open questions and verification](../play/cornhole.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- The back/forward cache: whether the browser keeps a Play match, and whether it comes back paused. A browser cannot be made to use the cache on demand ([Open questions and verification](../play/cornhole.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- An installed character without a connected rig. The test needs a pack made in Codex, and none ships with the repository; PSETUP-33 covers it when one is available ([Interactions with other systems](../play/cornhole.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).

Not checkable by hand:

- That saved bindings for players 3 and 4 are never read back. Any way of seeing slot 3's or 4's bindings changes its **Controls**, which resets them, so the effect cannot be observed ([Edge cases](../play/play-setup.md#edge-cases)).

- A lost WebGL context during a match. Play has no handler, and producing the loss needs a script ([Cancel and interrupt](../play/play-setup.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).

Not checkable by hand:

- Dodge always moves toward the front and wraps from the front lane to the back; in **Free steering** it is only a slide. Whether that is intended is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#open-questions-and-verification)). DASH-20 checks the behaviour.
- A half-pulled trigger pays the full stamina drain for part of the bonus. Whether that is fair is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#open-questions-and-verification)). DASH-22 checks the behaviour.
- Holding Sprint to empty leaves stamina near 5, below the 8 a jump needs. Whether that penalty is intended is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#open-questions-and-verification)). DASH-15 checks the behaviour.
- The cards' running "recovery" values (Dan 0.85, Doug 0.65) are unused. Whether they should matter is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#open-questions-and-verification)). DASH-06 checks the shared recovery rate.
- A dead heat goes to the lower-numbered player. Two runners crossing on the same 1/60 s game step cannot be produced on demand ([Edge cases](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#edge-cases)).
- The exact take-off and slide windows (for example 0.41–0.60 s before a hurdle at cruising speed) and the fastest-line times (11.6–11.9 s, or 12.3–12.5 s for a clean sprinting run) came from a re-implementation of the rules. They are too fine to time by hand ([While sprinting](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#while-sprinting)). DASH-09, DASH-10 and DASH-38 check the coarse behaviour.
- The AI's fixed 88-pixel reaction distance is read from the code; only its effect, occasional crashes, is visible ([Open questions and verification](../play/clubhouse-dash.md#open-questions-and-verification)). DASH-24 checks that effect.

Not checkable by hand:

- Counter stance is damage reduction, not a counterattack, and has no animation, effect or caption. Whether that is intended is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/backyard-brawl.md#open-questions-and-verification)). BRAWL-21 checks the behaviour.
- Setting up a grapple also starts counter stance, so it costs 12 more energy. Whether that is intended is a product call ([Open questions and verification](../play/backyard-brawl.md#open-questions-and-verification)). BRAWL-07 measures the cost.
- A draw reads **Draw** in the caption and **Session complete** in the result, and a guard break is announced as a block. Both are wording questions ([Open questions and verification](../play/backyard-brawl.md#open-questions-and-verification)); BRAWL-06 and BRAWL-16 observe them.
- A trade, both fighters' blows connecting on the same 1/60 s game step, cannot be produced on demand ([While attacking](../play/backyard-brawl.md#while-attacking)).
- A blow that connects on the very step the bout ends still counts. That step cannot be produced on demand ([Cancel and interrupt](../play/backyard-brawl.md#cancel-and-interrupt)).
- Presses made during hit-stop are read and wait in the buffer. The freeze lasts 0.05–0.08 s, too short to aim a press into by hand ([The hit](../play/backyard-brawl.md#the-hit)).
- Whether the drawn punch lines up with the moment the hit lands has not been measured. That needs frame-by-frame capture, not a hand check ([Open questions and verification](../play/backyard-brawl.md#open-questions-and-verification)).
- The damage each fighter deals is tallied internally but never shown or kept, so there is nothing to observe ([Interactions with other systems](../play/backyard-brawl.md#interactions-with-other-systems)).
